From new fraud to verified defence.

AEGISYNTH is an Autonomous Payment Defence Compiler: it safely simulates a novel payment attack, synthesizes a compact control, attacks that control for bypasses, repairs the weaknesses, and verifies the final policy before human review.

No real cards • No real merchants • No customer PII • No paid API • No automatic hard decline
